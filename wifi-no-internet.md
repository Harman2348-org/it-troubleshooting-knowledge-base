# Troubleshooting: Wi-Fi Connected but No Internet

**Status:** Draft — verify these steps on a device before marking this guide tested.

## Symptoms
The device shows that it is connected to Wi-Fi, but websites or online apps do not work.

## Questions to ask
- Are other devices on the same network affected?
- Does the issue occur on one website or every website?
- Did the router or internet service recently change?

## Diagnostic steps
1. Check whether other devices can access the internet.
2. Confirm Wi-Fi is enabled and the device is connected to the intended network.
3. Open more than one trusted website to rule out a single-site outage.
4. Restart the affected app or browser.
5. If other devices are also affected, check the router/modem status lights and the internet provider's outage information.
6. If only one device is affected, disconnect and reconnect to Wi-Fi.
7. Use the operating system's built-in network troubleshooter, if available.
8. If the problem remains, record the results and escalate to the network administrator or internet provider.

## Important
Do not factory-reset a router or change network settings without permission and a recovery plan.
